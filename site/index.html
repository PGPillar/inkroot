Root a BOOX with Magisk, safely

How it works

Three steps. Inkroot checks everything before it writes anything, and keeps a copy of your tablet's startup software so there's always a way back.

  1. Plug in

    Turn on USB debugging and connect your Note Max. Inkroot checks the model, the firmware and the battery.

  2. Safety copy

    Inkroot reads your tablet's startup software (the boot partition) over Qualcomm EDL, checks it twice, and keeps it on your computer. Nothing is written without it.

  3. Root

    Inkroot patches that copy on the tablet with Magisk 30.7, verified boot kept on, writes it back, reads it back to check it, and confirms root and the boot service are running.

Unbrick a BOOX · bootloop · black screen

BOOX stuck on the ONYX logo?

After a bad boot flash the tablet can look dead, hang on the logo or bootloop. That's almost always a soft brick. If you have a copy of its boot image, Inkroot's Rescue job walks you through the deep-flash cable step by step and writes it back.

BOOX fastboot removed

Why EDL? Onyx removed fastboot

That's why rooting a BOOX on firmware 4.2 goes through Qualcomm's Emergency Download mode (EDL): it sits below the bootloader, so it can make a safety copy and write it back even when the tablet won't start. Inkroot uses the open-source bkerler/edl client to do it.
